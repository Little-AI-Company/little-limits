The SVG sources are preserved here. The PNGs were rendered offline with
rsvg-convert at 96 by 96 pixels. AppKit displays them at 16 by 16 points
as template images, using their alpha silhouettes in the system appearance.
No SVG renderer or external dependency is needed at runtime or when packaging.

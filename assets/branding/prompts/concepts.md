## Style reference

The attached images are the sibling products (Little Worker's Pip with the TV head, and Little CI's runner with the server-box head). Match their look closely:
- Thick, confident, even black outlines with round ends. Flat fills. Characters are black and white only.
- A cute character whose head is an everyday object, with the family face: two solid black oval eyes and a small smile.
- Black ribbed turtleneck sweater body.
- A soft flat pastel circle behind the character. Warm cream background (#FBFAF7 to #F9F7F2).
- No gradients, no glow, no gloss, no 3D, no shading, no texture, no extra colors beyond the one pastel accent.
- Leave lots of empty space. Calm, kind, still.

## The character

Its head is a round dial gauge, like a friendly fuel gauge, with a thick black rim. The face sits in the middle of the dial. A few short tick marks run along the top edge and one short needle points low and to the left, so a little is left. Keep the needle small so it does not cross the face.

Pastel accent for Little Limits: soft butter yellow (#F6ECC6 range). It must be clearly different from Little Worker's sand and Little CI's mint.

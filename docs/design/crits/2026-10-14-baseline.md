# Design crit 2026-10-14 baseline

`UX diagnostic 0/10 · HIG 4 pass, 11 fail, 10 untested, 0 N/A · Changes required · Worst open severity 3`

Revision bb445d3, clean product source. macOS 27.0.1 (26A434), Apple silicon, packaged Release build, 2x display, demo fixtures. Method: the repository [crit skill](../../../.agents/skills/crit/SKILL.md), with independent UX heuristic (Krug, Nielsen) and Apple HIG audits merged into one register. Captures and the annotated HTML report stay in ignored `.crit/2026-10-14-baseline/`.

## Verdict

Changes required. Worst open severity 3.

## What a first-time user experiences

The features are all there and the data is honest underneath. The problem is presentation: the same fact is shown in different ways in different places, and the most important numbers are the hardest to read.

Glance at the status menu: every reading is drawn in the dimmed gray macOS uses for disabled items, and it says "49% used" while the sidebar says "51% left" for the same quota. An account that needs sign-in still shows normal-looking percentages, and the fix is not in the menu.

Open the window: half of Home is a mostly empty two-week calendar. The table that answers the real question sits underneath, cut off, scrolling both ways, with columns truncated mid-word. Low quota is shown in pale green, orange, or red text that fails contrast in light mode.

The window itself predates macOS 26: no toolbar, a hand-built sidebar that cannot hide or float on Liquid Glass, controls laid out as buttons in the content, and Settings behind an unlabeled gear at the bottom. Labels change from screen to screen (earned resets, quota resets; model limit, Fable 5 weekly; five names for API credits).

## Proposed fix order

- **1. Make the status menu readable and honest.** One PR in UsageMenu and InterfaceFormatting. Biggest daily impact: remaining everywhere, enabled reading rows that open the account, state and Reconnect under the heading, one earned-resets row, no placeholder credit rows. (MENU-01, MENU-02, MENU-03, MENU-04, MENU-05, MENU-06)
- **2. Fix the Home layout.** Table first at full height in one scroll view, columns that fit, calendar events phrased as resets, color backed by words and labelColor numbers. (HOME-01, HOME-02, HOME-07, HOME-10, HOME-11)
- **3. Adopt macOS 26 window chrome.** NSSplitViewController sidebar on Liquid Glass, unified NSToolbar with three groups and menu commands, Settings in the status and app menus, click-only popover. Guard nothing: these APIs exist on macOS 14 and adopt glass automatically on 26. (HOME-03, HOME-04, HOME-06, SETTINGS-01)
- **4. One vocabulary.** Write a short glossary in NATIVE-DESIGN.md and apply it to every string; fix the empty state, error text, and add-account flow. (HOME-08, HOME-05, HOME-09, EMPTY-01, PLATFORM-01, ADD-ACCOUNT-01, ADD-ACCOUNT-02, ERROR-01, MENU-07, MENU-08, ACCOUNT-01, ACCOUNT-02, ACCOUNT-03)

## Correct as designed

- En dashes in calendar date ranges ("Oct 12 – 25") are correct range typography; the project bans em dashes only.
- Gray account section headers are the documented native NSMenuItem.sectionHeader exception.
- Disabled Refresh, Refresh All, Reconnect…, and add-account controls are demo-mode behavior, and the "Demo" row and "Example accounts" footer appear only in demo.
- Add and remove square buttons directly under the account list follow the native source-list convention with AX labels.
- All measured text is at or above the 10 pt macOS minimum and measured controls are at least 20 pt tall.
- No continuous animation or countdown repaint timer; the only timer is the 300 s refresh.
- "Remaining percentages from the latest reading · Times in CDT" is label-color explanation below its heading, not a gray subtitle above a section.
- Remove confirmation names provider and identity and pairs Remove with Cancel; removal is a deliberate choice, so the destructive style is optional.

## Limits

- The shell had neither Accessibility nor synthetic input permission and computer-use was unavailable, so only launch-flag surfaces were captured. Account page, App Settings, sheets, and Platform findings are source evidence.
- Status menu captures show the dark system menu appearance; the app's `--dark-appearance` flag affects windows only.
- One Home capture shows the calendar popover, opened by the resting pointer during capture.
- `--demo-empty` and `--demo-load-error` require `--demo`; four early launches without it loaded the real saved accounts. Those captures were deleted, and the skill's surface inventory now passes `--demo`.
